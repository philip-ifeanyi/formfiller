FF-08 Dynamic Form Resilience Fixture

This page is a permanent regression surface for delayed rendering and stale-element replacement during a fill run.

How To Use

  1. Load the extension build.
  2. Click the in-page Fill Form button on each fixture.
  3. Confirm the replacement email field still finishes with a value and the delayed phone field appears and fills on a later pass.

Dynamic Replace Fixture

The email input is replaced shortly after the first input event. FF-08 should recover on a retry and keep the expected value on the replacement node.

Delayed Discovery Fixture

The phone field is inserted after the first name field begins filling. FF-08 should rescan, discover the new field, and fill it before the session ends.
